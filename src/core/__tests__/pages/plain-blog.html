Nan's pumpkin soup

Every winter Nan made this soup. Here are my thoughts on pumpkins, at length.

Serves 6. Ready in under an hour.

Ingredients

Soup

  • 1 kg butternut pumpkin, peeled and chopped
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 1 L chicken stock

To serve

  • 1/2 cup cream
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Method

  1. 1. Fry the onion and garlic in a large pot until soft.
  2. Add pumpkin and stock, simmer 20 minutes.
  3. Blend until smooth and stir through the cream.
