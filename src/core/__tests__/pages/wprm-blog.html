Ingredients in red velvet cheesecake

Here's what you need. Some notes on the ingredients:

  • Cream cheese – use blocks, not spreadable.
  • Food colouring – gel is best.

How to make it

Step one is easy. Photos below!

  1. Look at this photo of the batter.

Red Velvet Cheesecake

Ingredients

Red velvet cake

  • ▢1 1/4 cups plain flour (all-purpose)
  • ▢1 tbsp cocoa powder
  • ▢1/2 tsp baking soda (bi-carb)
  • ▢3/4 cup caster sugar (superfine)
  • ▢1/2 cup buttermilk
  • ▢1 tbsp red food colouring (Note 1)

Cheesecake

  • 500 g cream cheese, softened
  • 2/3 cup caster sugar
  • 3 large eggs, at room temperature
  • 1/2 cup sour cream

Frosting:

  • 125 g cream cheese, softened
  • 1 1/2 cups icing sugar
  • Pinch of salt

Instructions

Red velvet cake

  • Preheat oven to 160°C/320°F (140°C fan).
  • Whisk flour, cocoa and baking soda. Add the rest and mix until smooth.

Notes

1. Red food colouring – gel colouring gives the deepest red.

2. Storage – keeps for 5 days in the fridge.
