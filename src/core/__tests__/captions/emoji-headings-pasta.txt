Creamy Tuscan Chicken Pasta 🍝🔥 ready in 20 minutes!

This is my go-to weeknight dinner, the whole family loves it 😍

Serves 4 | Prep 10 mins | Cook 20 mins

INGREDIENTS 👇
▪️ 400g penne
▪️ 2 chicken breasts, diced
▪️ 3 garlic cloves, minced
▪️ 1/2 cup sun-dried tomatoes
▪️ 300ml thickened cream
▪️ 2 handfuls baby spinach
▪️ 50g parmesan, grated
▪️ Salt and pepper

METHOD 👇
1️⃣ Cook the pasta in salted water until al dente.
2️⃣ Season the chicken and pan fry until golden, then set aside.
3️⃣ Add garlic and sun-dried tomatoes to the pan and cook for 1 minute.
4️⃣ Pour in the cream and parmesan and simmer for 3 minutes.
5️⃣ Stir through the spinach, chicken and pasta.
