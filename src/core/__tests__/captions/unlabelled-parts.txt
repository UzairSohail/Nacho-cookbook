Honey garlic salmon bites 🍯🐟

Salmon:
500g salmon fillets, cubed
1 tbsp cornflour
Salt & pepper

Glaze:
3 tbsp honey
2 tbsp soy sauce
3 garlic cloves, grated
1 tsp chilli flakes (optional)

Toss the salmon in cornflour, salt and pepper.
Pan fry in a little oil for 6-8 minutes until golden on all sides.
Mix the glaze ingredients, pour over the salmon and let it bubble for 1-2 minutes until sticky.
Serve with rice and greens!
