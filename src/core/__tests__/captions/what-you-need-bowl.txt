HIGH PROTEIN BURRITO BOWL 🌯
Only 520 calories and 48g protein!

What you'll need:
- 500g lean beef mince
- 1 packet taco seasoning
- 1 cup microwave rice
- 1 can black beans, drained
- 1 avocado
- Juice of 1 lime
- Light sour cream, to serve

How to make it:
- Brown the mince in a hot pan, breaking it up as it cooks
- Add taco seasoning and a splash of water and simmer for 5 mins
- Heat the rice and beans
- Build your bowls and top with avocado, lime and sour cream

Calories: 520 | Protein: 48g | Carbs: 45g | Fat: 16g
