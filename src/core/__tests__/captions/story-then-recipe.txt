Honestly these are the best brownies I have ever made and I'm not even exaggerating. Fudgy middle, crinkly top, done in one bowl. My kids demolished the whole tray in a day 😂

Ingredients (makes 16):
200g dark chocolate
150g butter
1 cup caster sugar
3 eggs
3/4 cup plain flour
1/4 cup cocoa powder
Pinch of salt

Melt the chocolate and butter together in the microwave in 30 second bursts.
Whisk in the sugar, then the eggs one at a time.
Fold through the flour, cocoa and salt until just combined.
Bake at 170°C for 25 minutes. Let cool completely before slicing!

Notes: They firm up overnight in the fridge.
