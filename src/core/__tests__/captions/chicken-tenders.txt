Raising Cane's Style Chicken 🍗🌶️
140g Protein! 💪

(Macros: 3 Tenders, Fries & 45g Sauce)
964 Calories
75gC | 30gF | 140gP

Compared to the fast food version:
1,250 Calories | 61g Protein 😮

Ingredients: (Chicken)
1.1KG Boneless & Skinless Chicken Breast (butterflied & cut into 6 strips)
1 Tsp Smoked Paprika, 1/2 Tsp Cayenne Pepper, 1/2 Tsp Black Pepper, 1 Tsp Salt & 1 Tsp Garlic Powder
100g Potato Starch
200g Plain Flour
Seasoning Flour With Same Seasonings Above
1 Cup Sparkling/Soda Water
Light Cooking Oil Spray

Ingredients: (Raising Cane’s Sauce)
350g Light Mayonnaise (Heinz Light Mayonnaise)
170g Low Sugar Ketchup (Heinz 50% Less Added Sugar Ketchup)
45g Worcestershire Sauce
1 Tsp Garlic Powder
1.5 Tsp Salt
1/2 Tsp Onion Powder
1 Tsp Black Pepper

Ingredients: (Fries)
400g Carisma Potatoes
1 Tsp Salt
1 Tsp Smoked Paprika
Light Cooking Oil Spray

1. Butterfly & cut the chicken into thin strips, season and mix well.
2. Coat chicken in potato starch, dip into sparkling water, then coat generously in the seasoned flour. Press the flour firmly into each piece so it sticks.
3. Place chicken onto a lined tray or into the air fryer, spray evenly with cooking oil and air fry until golden & crispy, flipping halfway through and spraying any dry areas.
4. Cut and season the potatoes, spray with cooking oil and air fry until golden & crispy.
5. Mix all sauce ingredients together and refrigerate to thicken.
6. Serve 3 tenders with fries and 45g sauce per serving.

Important Notes:
- This makes 6 tenders, but the macros are based on 3 tenders + fries + 45g sauce.
- Make sure there are no dry flour patches before cooking and spray the chicken evenly with oil for the best crispy finish.
