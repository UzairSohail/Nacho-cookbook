Garlic butter prawns 🦐 (serves 2)

Ingredients:
Prawns, 500g
Butter - 3 tbsp
Garlic, 4 cloves
Parsley, handful
Lemon, 1/2

Directions:
Step 1 - Melt the butter in a pan over medium heat.
Step 2 - Add garlic and cook for 30 seconds.
Step 3 - Add prawns and cook 2 mins each side.
Step 4 - Finish with parsley and lemon juice.

Tip: Don't overcook the prawns or they go rubbery!
