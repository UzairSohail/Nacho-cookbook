5 Minute Peanut Noodles

Ingredients: 2 nests egg noodles, 2 tbsp peanut butter, 1 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tsp sriracha, 1 tsp honey, spring onion to serve

Method: Cook the noodles. Whisk the peanut butter, soy, sriracha and honey with 3 tbsp of the hot noodle water, then toss through the noodles. Top with spring onion.
