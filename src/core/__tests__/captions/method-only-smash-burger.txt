✨ Double Patty Chicken Smash Burger 🍔 made extra cheesy with D’lecta Chilli Cheese 🧀🌶️

Chicken Smash Burger – Method

1. In a bowl, add 250 g finely minced boneless chicken.
2. Add 1 tsp red chilli powder, ½ tsp black pepper powder, 1 tsp chicken masala and salt to taste.
3. Mix everything well until the seasoning is evenly combined with the chicken. Do not overmix.
4. Divide the mixture into 2 portions and shape each portion loosely into a ball.
5. Heat a little oil in a heavy-bottomed pan over medium-high heat.
6. Place one chicken ball onto the hot pan and immediately press it firmly with a spatula to make a thin smash patty.
7. Cook until the bottom turns golden and crispy, then flip and cook the other side until the chicken is completely cooked through.
8. Place 1 Cheese Slice on top of the hot chicken patty, cover the pan briefly and let the cheese melt.
9. Repeat the same process with the remaining chicken mixture.

Caramelized Onions

1. Thinly slice 1 large onion.
2. Heat 1 tbsp butter in a pan and add the sliced onions.
3. Add a pinch of salt and sauté on medium-low heat.
4. Add ½ tsp sugar and continue cooking until the onions become soft, golden brown and beautifully caramelized. Stir occasionally to prevent burning.

Burger Sauce

1. In a bowl, add 4 tbsp mayonnaise, 1 tbsp mustard sauce and 2 tbsp ketchup
2. Add ½ tsp red chili powder, ½ tsp garlic powder, ½ tsp sugar and salt to taste.
3. Mix everything until smooth and creamy.

Assembly

1. Slice the burger buns and toast them with a little butter until golden.
2. Spread the prepared burger sauce generously on the bottom bun.
3. Add a generous layer of caramelized onions.
4. Place the Cheese Slice-topped chicken smash patty over the sauce.
5. Add lettuce on top.
6. Add more burger sauce and caramelized onions.
7. Close with the top bun and serve hot.
